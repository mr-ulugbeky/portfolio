Questions
Quick answers.
Do I need to book in advance?

Booking ahead guarantees your slot and your preferred barber. Walk-ins are welcome if we have availability, but busy periods — especially weekends — fill up fast. We recommend booking at least a day ahead.

Can I request a specific barber?

Absolutely. When you book online or by phone, just let us know who you'd like. We'll do our best to match you, and if your barber isn't available, we'll suggest the closest slot.

What if I'm not happy with my cut?

Tell us before you leave. If anything is off, we fix it right then — no charge, no argument. Our goal is that you walk out satisfied. If you notice something later, contact us and we'll sort it out.

Do you do kids' haircuts?

Yes, for children under 12. We're patient and used to working with kids. Mention it when you book so we allocate the right time and barber for your appointment.

Where are you located?

We're at 18 Amir Temur Avenue, Tashkent. There's street parking in front and we're a short walk from the Amir Temur Hiyoboni metro station.
